Questions we hear often

Something missing? Call us on (555) 010-0123 and we will answer it.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are the fastest way to be seen, and most patients book online. We keep a few same-day openings each morning for urgent but non-emergency concerns. Call us and we will do our best to fit you in.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Choose New patient in the booking form and we will set aside a longer first visit so your doctor can review your history properly.

What should I bring to my visit?

Bring your health card or insurance details, photo ID, a list of current medications and supplements, and any recent test results or referral letters.

Do you offer virtual visits?

Yes. Virtual visits suit prescription renewals, test result reviews, follow-ups and many minor concerns. Your doctor will tell you if you need to come in person.

How do I cancel or change my appointment?

Call us or reply to your confirmation message at least 24 hours before your visit so we can offer the time to another patient.

What if I have a medical emergency?

Call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the US) or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not use the booking form for emergencies.
